# Workflow: Backlink / Authority Strategy

**Purpose:** Recommend ethical, sustainable ways to build off-site authority. This workflow produces strategy and recommendations only — this skill does not and should not directly execute outreach, publish on third-party sites, or purchase links.

**Modifies files:** No, beyond possibly creating genuinely useful on-site assets (e.g., original research content) that would also serve `workflows/content-strategy.md`.

## Ethical foundation

Authority earned through genuinely useful, citation-worthy assets and real relationships is durable. Authority purchased or manufactured through schemes is fragile and carries real penalty risk (see [[../rules/no-black-hat]]). This workflow only recommends the former.

## Legitimate strategies to recommend

- **Linkable assets** — original research, data studies, genuinely useful free tools/calculators, comprehensive guides that don't exist elsewhere at this quality. These earn links because people want to cite them, not because anyone was asked to link.
- **Digital PR** — pitching genuinely newsworthy findings, data, or expert commentary to relevant journalists/publications.
- **Relevant, effortful guest content** — contributing genuinely valuable content to industry publications the site has a real reason to be featured in, not mass-distributed generic posts.
- **Resource page inclusion** — identifying existing curated resource/link pages in the site's genuine niche where inclusion would be earned on merit.
- **Genuine partnerships** — co-marketing, integrations, or collaborations with real business relationships that naturally produce mutual links.
- **Community participation** — genuine, valuable participation in relevant communities, forums, and industry discussions (not link-dropping; participation that happens to be linkable because it's useful).
- **Unlinked mention reclamation** — finding existing mentions of the brand/site that aren't yet linked and reasonably requesting a link, since this is a low-friction, legitimate ask.

## Explicitly prohibited

Per [[../rules/no-black-hat]]: PBNs, link farms, paid links without disclosure (`rel="sponsored"`), automated reciprocal link exchanges at scale, comment/forum spam, mass low-effort guest posting, expired-domain link schemes, any scheme whose primary purpose is manipulating ranking signals rather than earning genuine citation.

## Evaluating link quality (for competitor/opportunity analysis)

When identifying link targets, prioritize genuine topical relevance and real audience overlap over raw domain authority metrics alone — a highly relevant link from a smaller, real site in the same niche is usually more valuable and safer than an irrelevant link from a large but unrelated domain.

## Output

A prioritized list of legitimate authority-building opportunities and linkable-asset ideas, feeding `templates/content-roadmap.md` where the recommendation is itself a content asset. Do not present this as a guarantee of specific link acquisition — it's a strategy, not a promise of outcome.
